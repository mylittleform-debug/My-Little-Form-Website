About Us

My Little Form started in January 2026 with the goal of creating handmade personalized keepsakes that capture meaningful memories through clay art.

Every product is carefully sculpted and crafted to reflect the personality, story, and emotion behind each order.

Aside from personalized clay figures, we also introduced My Little Charm, a personalized clay keychain collection designed for people who want smaller but meaningful handmade pieces they can bring anywhere.

How To Pre-Order?

1. Message Us

Message us on Facebook, TikTok, or Instagram.

2. Send Your Reference Photo

Send your reference or inspiration photo and we will provide the price for your personalized figure.

3. Pay 50% Downpayment

This will reserve your slot and schedule the starting day of the sculpting process. Please note that the reserved slot is for the starting/working day of the sculpting process, not the ship-out date.

4. Wait for Your Scheduled Sculpting Day

Usually takes 5–7 working days. We will send updates during the process.

5. Order Completed

Pay remaining balance + shipping fee.

6. Shipment & Delivery

Your figure will be shipped to you.
